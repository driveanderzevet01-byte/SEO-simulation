Understanding SEO Components

Off-Page SEO

Company Name & Description: The brand identity. Establishes trust and authority for search engines.

Product Keywords: The exact terms your target audience types into search engines.

On-Page SEO

Product Name, Link & Headline: The main title and URL structure. Must contain primary keywords.

Ad Description: The meta description. Compelling and uses keywords naturally.

Promo Theme & Discount: Visual hooks and incentives that increase Click-Through Rate (CTR).
